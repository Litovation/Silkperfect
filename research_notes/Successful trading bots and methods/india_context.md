## Q1. SEBI studies on retail trader outcomes (F&O series 2022-2026, intraday cash study, algo/HFT findings)

### Takeaway
SEBI's F&O studies show roughly 89% to 93% of individual traders losing money every year since FY22. The newest study (released Aug 2026, FY26) shows 87.7% of individuals losing, aggregate net losses of Rs 91,685 crore (first year-on-year fall), and about Rs 44,483 crore of proprietary-desk gross profit, with 99% of FPI and prop profit from algo entities. The project report's "Rs 61,000 crore" figure matches the FY24 prop plus FPI total, not FY25, so it should be re-labelled.

## Q2. SEBI retail algo trading framework (Feb 2025 circular, NSE implementation, static IP, registration, OPS limit, white/black box, status Nov 2026)

### Takeaway
SEBI's circular of 4 Feb 2025 plus NSE's implementation circular (NSE/INVG/67858, 5-6 May 2025) became fully mandatory for brokers on 1 Apr 2026. A self-coding retail person must use a whitelisted static IP per API key (one change per week), stay under the 10 orders/second cap unless the algo is exchange-registered, and orders carry exchange algo IDs. Third-party strategy sellers must be exchange-empanelled providers, and "black box" providers must also be SEBI Research Analysts.

### Cited Findings

**Source circulars and timeline**
- SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013, 4 Feb 2025, "Safer participation of retail investors in algorithmic trading": brokers become principals for retail algos including those from third-party vendors; applies to API-based retail algos; exchanges to amend bye-laws; industry standards forum to finalise standards by 1 Apr 2025. — [SEBI circular (snippet)](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/feb-2025/safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading_91614.html); [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/sebi-introduces-new-framework-for-safer-algo-trading-for-investors/76294.html)

**Static IP and rate limit**
- Static IP is required for API-based order placement from 1 Apr 2026 (Zerodha says regardless of order frequency, for all Kite Connect order-API users); changing the IP allowed about once per calendar week; Zerodha and Upstox say limits apply per account. — [Zerodha support](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/general-kite/kite-api/articles/static-ip); [Kite forum thread on compliance prep](https://www.kite.trade/forum/discussion/comment/52157); [Upstox community, 1 Apr 2026 mandates](https://community.upstox.com/t/important-new-sebi-exchange-mandates-for-api-trading-effective-1st-april-2026/14822)

**What it means by route (my reading of cited facts, see Inferences)**
- Own code on own machine via broker API (Kite Connect, Upstox, Dhan, SmartAPI, Fyers, OpenAlgo calling broker API): static IP whitelisted at broker, stay under 10 OPS, broker tags orders with a generic algo ID.
- Platform that runs on the broker's/provider's servers (Streak, Tradetron etc.): provider empanelment and (for black-box) RA registration sit with the provider; the user does not need own static IP if the provider's/broker's IP is used.

### Inferences
- A small team running its own strategies at low frequency (monthly or quarterly rebalance, as the project's momentum test) needs only: a static IP (cloud VM or ISP static IP), broker whitelisting, and keeping under 10 OPS. No exchange algo registration is needed at that frequency. The compliance burden is operational, not a legal bar.
- OpenAlgo is self-hosted software that relays to the broker API, so the static IP rules apply to wherever it is hosted; it is not an empanelled provider (no evidence found). If the team shares OpenAlgo access with outsiders or sells signals, it would risk crossing into provider/RA territory, which this research did not resolve.
- Intraday momentum-breakout style bots at the project's 5-minute frequency would stay far below 10 OPS, so the OPS cap is not binding for them.

### Gaps
- Primary text of the 4 Feb 2025 circular, the 30 Sep 2025 circular, and NSE/INVG/67858 not opened. Exact penalty and enforcement behaviour for non-compliance not confirmed.
- No official confirmation of status after 1 Apr 2026 (any further relaxations or amendments, e.g. 2026 circulars) was found; it is an absence of search hits, not proof.
- Per-broker number of IPs allowed and cost of static IPs (dev.to figure of Rs 200-500 per month is unverified).
- Whether self-hosted OpenAlgo is treated as "tech-savvy investor API use" vs needing provider status is not covered by any source I found.
- Whether black-box vs white-box registration applies to a team that only trades its own money: the cited material addresses providers to retail clients, not own-account use.

## Q5. Taxes and costs for algo traders

### Takeaway
STT on F&O rose on 1 Apr 2026 (futures 0.02% to 0.05% on sell; options premium 0.10% to 0.15%); capital gains rates are STCG 20%, LTCG 12.5% above Rs 1.25 lakh. F&O (and intraday) income is business income taxed at slab rates, loss carry-forward 8 years, possible tax audit by turnover. Higher costs and tax friction argue against high-frequency trading at retail size.

### Cited Findings
- Budget 2026-27 (1 Feb 2026) raised STT on futures from 0.02% to 0.05% and on options (premium) from 0.10% to 0.15%, effective 1 Apr 2026; exercised-option STT now 0.15% (earlier 0.125%/0.13%, sources differ); delivery and intraday equity STT unchanged. Government said aim was to curb speculation. — [Business Today, 26 Mar 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/story/stt-hike-on-fo-trades-to-take-effect-from-april-1-trading-costs-to-rise-522428-2026-03-26); [Business Standard, 1 Feb 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/markets-fm-hikes-stt-on-fo-trades-surprise-move-126020100888_1.html); [Upstox explainer](https://upstox.com/news/personal-finance/tax/explained-how-the-stt-hike-on-equity-futures-and-options-affects-traders-and-investors/article-189260)
- Capital gains on listed equity in FY2026-27: STCG 20%, LTCG 12.5% above Rs 1.25 lakh exemption, unchanged in Budget 2026. — [Business Standard, 1 Feb 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/budget/news/budget-2026-ltcg-tax-rates-unchanged-for-fy27-fm-sitharaman-126020100424_1.html); [Univest](https://univest.in/blogs/capital-gains-tax-on-shares-fy-2026-27-stcg-ltcg-stt) (one source mis-dated the STCG hike; the 15%-to-20% change was in the 2024 budget per other sources)
- F&O income is non-speculative business income at slab rates, filed in ITR-3; losses carry forward up to 8 years; presumptive scheme (6% of turnover) via ITR-4 is mentioned as an option in some sources. — [Sahi](https://www.sahi.com/blogs/income-tax-on-f-and-o-trading-in-india-complete-guide-for-fy-2025-26); [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/tax-filing-security-traders)
- Tax audit trigger is turnover-based (Rs 10 crore if cash receipts and payments are 5% or less, else lower thresholds); F&O turnover = absolute sum of profits and losses plus option sell premium; sources conflict on penalty size; a new Income-tax Act 2025 renumbering was mentioned. **[conflict, secondary tax blogs]** — [CA Rajput](https://carajput.com/blog/tax-audit-applicability-for-fo-traders-ay-2026-27/); [Business Today, Sep 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/tax/story/fo-trader-shock-why-even-your-losses-could-trigger-a-rs15-lakh-audit-penalty-494983-2025-09-21)
- Experts expected the STT hike to have limited long-run effect on volumes; SEBI data shows options volumes down over 50% in FY26. — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/stt-on-derivatives-raised-from-apr-1-experts-see-limited-impact-despite-higher-costs); [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/sebi-report-shows-over-50-drop-in-options-trading-volumes-in-fy26-after-f-o-reforms)

### Inferences
- A cost wall of about 0.25% per round trip in the project's day-trading test is consistent with delivery/intraday STT of about 0.1% on cash plus brokerage, exchange fees, stamp duty and slippage (components not verified here).
- For cash equity momentum rebalanced quarterly, holdings above 12 months qualify for LTCG 12.5% above Rs 1.25 lakh; short-term holdings are taxed 20%. Intraday cash trades are business (speculative) income, not capital gains (standard Indian tax treatment, not sourced in this research).
- Tax treatment strongly favours less-frequent, longer-holding strategies.

### Gaps
- GST rate on brokerage (understood to be 18%), SEBI turnover fee, exchange transaction charges, stamp duty rates, and cash-segment STT (0.1% delivery/sell-side intraday) were not retrieved from sources, so are not stated as facts.
- Current audit threshold under the new Income-tax Act 2025 and its section numbers not confirmed from an official source.
- Speculative vs non-speculative classification of intraday equity not confirmed from a retrieved source.
